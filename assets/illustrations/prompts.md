# Illustration sources

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, `transparent_background: true`.
The existing `assets/branding/logo_source.png` was the style reference.
Outputs were resized to at most 512 px and encoded as WebP with alpha preserved.
These are decorative educational illustrations, not diagnostic images.

## hero.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Create a production transparent PNG illustration for My Eyes Flutter app home hero and welcome onboarding. Subject: a friendly large almond-shaped illustrated eye with peach eyelid rim, ivory sclera, iris left blue right golden amber and navy pupil, accompanied by two small rounded colored swatch cards and a tiny sparkle. Refine the attached logo into a small joyful educational vignette, not a new logo. Match clean hand-drawn digital 2D contours, restrained flat solid palette, very subtle cel shading. Navy #141E35 outlines, ivory #F5F7FD, blue, golden amber #E0A32E, peach. Compact horizontal composition, large readable shapes; artwork legible at 120px. Transparent margins around entire subject. NO text, letters, numbers, photographic texture, 3D, bevels, gradients, airbrush or drop shadows. Genuine transparent background. Input image is style reference only.
